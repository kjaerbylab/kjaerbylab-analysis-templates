# Authorship and reuse decisions

This is a draft assembly of user-supplied code and code from the user's thesis repository. Original notices are retained. No blanket MIT or other license has been imposed on code whose rights have not been established.

Before public release, confirm permission/license for each contributed implementation and third-party dependency with its author or rights holder. Record the decision and attribution in its module README. Method citation does not replace software author credit. Repository ownership or filenames alone do not establish per-file authorship.

New documentation and synthetic examples were prepared with OpenAI Codex assistance on 2026-09-28. The lab should assign maintainers and choose appropriate terms for its own contributions.
